Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

1. Who we are

Hiba ("Hiba", "we", "us") is an AI-assisted tool that helps professionals turn their ideas into posts for LinkedIn, X and Substack. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Contact for all privacy questions: techpath411@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider, never in plain text). If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email from Google.

Profile and onboarding answers: your name, profession, goals, audience, tone, topics, platforms, posting frequency, approval preference, and any writing samples you choose to share.

Your content: ideas you type or speak, transcripts, drafts, posts and schedules.

Connected accounts: if you connect LinkedIn or X, the access tokens and profile identifiers needed to post on your behalf, and basic performance figures where the platform provides them.

Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and logs used for security and troubleshooting.

3. Voice input

This version of Hiba does not store audio recordings. Voice typing uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser (for example Chrome), your audio may be sent to the browser provider's speech service to be converted to text, under that provider's privacy policy. Only the resulting text reaches Hiba. You can use Hiba without voice by typing instead.

4. How we use your information

To create and secure your account; to generate drafts in your voice; to schedule and publish posts only as you approve or configure; to provide support; to keep the service safe and working; to improve Hiba; and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content to train AI models ourselves.

5. Service providers we rely on

Supabase (hosting, database and authentication); OpenAI (generating drafts from your ideas); Resend (sending verification and password emails); and LinkedIn, X and Substack only when you connect or publish to them. Your content is sent to our AI provider to produce drafts, under its own terms. At the time of writing, content sent through its business API is not used to train its models by default; please review its current policy. Each provider processes data only to deliver its service to us.

6. Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR or the Nigeria Data Protection Act apply, we process data to perform our agreement with you, with your consent (for example when you connect an account), for our legitimate interests in running a secure service, and to comply with the law.

7. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the European Union and the United States. We rely on our providers' contractual safeguards for these transfers.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it, and from backups within 90 days. Connected-account tokens are removed when you disconnect.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your information, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential browser storage to keep you signed in and to keep unsaved drafts on your device. We do not use advertising cookies.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure you can only access your own data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you we will notify you as the law requires.

12. Children

Hiba is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes we will update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
